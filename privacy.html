Privacy Policy — MUTALIX

Last updated: 6 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game MUTALIX (the “Game”) handles information. The Game is developed and published by Hafenente, a sole proprietorship registered to Swen Grünwald, Grindelallee 110, 20146 Hamburg, Germany (“we”, “us”). By installing or using the Game you agree to this Policy.

1. The short version

  • We keep data collection to what the Game needs to run, save your progress, show ads, and stay stable.
  • Your game progress can be backed up to the cloud under an anonymous, device-generated ID — we never ask for your name, email, or a login.
  • The Game uses Google AdMob for ads and Google Firebase for cloud save, analytics, crash reporting, and remote configuration.
  • Analytics and crash reporting are OFF until you consent. In the EEA/UK you see a consent dialog; on iOS you also see Apple’s tracking prompt.
  • The Game is not directed to children under 16 (or the minimum age in your country).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the Game free we display ads through Google AdMob. To serve, cap, and measure ads, Google’s SDK may process your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, approximate (IP-based) location, device/app information, and ad-interaction events. Whether ads are personalized depends on your consent and tracking choices (Section 5). For details see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from AdMob apps.

4. Cloud save (Firebase)

So your progress survives a reinstall or new device, we can store it in Google Firebase (Firestore) under an anonymous ID generated on your device. This backup contains game progress only — no name, email, or login. Analytics and Crashlytics send diagnostic data only after you consent (Section 5). Firebase is a Google service; see the Firebase Privacy and Security page.

5. Your consent & tracking choices

  • EEA / UK (GDPR): on first launch we show a Google-certified consent dialog (Google’s User Messaging Platform) covering ads and analytics. You can re-open it anytime via Settings → Privacy & Legal → Manage Consent.
  • iOS App Tracking Transparency: the Game asks for tracking permission. If you decline, ads are non-personalized. Change it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Analytics & crash reporting default to OFF and only begin after you consent. Withdraw consent anytime via Manage Consent — collection stops going forward.

6. Data retention & deletion

On-device data is removed when you uninstall, or via Settings → Reset Progress. Analytics data is retained by Google for up to 14 months. To delete your cloud save, contact us with your request (the anonymous ID is available in the Game’s support/settings screen). For ad-related data use the controls in Section 5 and Google My Ad Center.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR (EEA/UK) you have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and port your personal data, and to object to processing and withdraw consent; under the CCPA/CPRA (California) you may access, delete, and opt out of “sharing” for personalized ads. Exercise these via the in-app controls above or by emailing us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in our case the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Hamburgische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit).

8. International transfers

Google and Apple may process data in countries outside the EEA, including the United States, under the safeguards described in their policies (e.g. EU Standard Contractual Clauses / the EU–US Data Privacy Framework).

9. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 16 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will address it.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as the Game evolves or as required by law. Material changes are reflected by the “Last updated” date above.
